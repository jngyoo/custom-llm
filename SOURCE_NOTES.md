# Sources and assistance

The base corpus is the synthetic classroom generator from [pepealonso95/custom-llm](https://github.com/pepealonso95/custom-llm), used through the course notebook. nanoGPT attribution and its MIT license are preserved. No private documents or scraped third-party articles were added to the teaching corpus.

The six added files are AI-assisted original synthetic teaching examples created during this assignment. They contain fictional/general everyday situations and are included here for reproduction. The first two were developed after public evaluation categories and example cases were consulted; exact prompt matching was used to check separation. The later four were ordinary topic-based additions. This provenance limits any claim of unseen-test generalization; exact-match checks do not establish semantic independence. The benchmark files were not imported into the corpus.

AI assistance was also used to explain the notebook, organize the submitted files, draft the English analysis from recorded results, and run verification. The submitted scores, vectors, chat outputs and training artifacts come from the actual saved experiments. No new training was performed during submission preparation. Transient Colab execution-account metadata was removed from notebook copies; code, saved outputs and execution counts were retained.
